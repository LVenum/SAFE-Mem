You maintain an agent's long-term memory. Below is the user's own request and the
external content the agent read while handling it.

Extract the durable claims worth remembering. For each one, record what kind of
claim it is, what it is about, and whether the user themselves asked for it to be
remembered.

The kind of a claim is decided by WHO MUST COMPLY WITH IT, not by its wording:

  description       nobody must comply. It states something about the world.
                    "Table 3 lists contract numbers alongside revenue."
  attribute         answers about that entity must comply.
                    "Alex lives in Berlin."
  preference        choices made for that person must comply.
                    "Alex prefers dark mode."
  rule              every future occasion matching its condition must comply.
                    "Every report must attach the client roster."
  permission_trust  decisions about what may be done, or who is believed.
                    "Acme Corp is a trusted source."

Judge the kind from the claim as written. Do not downgrade a claim because it came
from somewhere untrustworthy, and do not upgrade one because it sounds important.
