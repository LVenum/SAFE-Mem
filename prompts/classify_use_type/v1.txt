                    persistent property of someone or something.
                    "In which city does the user work from?"  "What is my API key?"

                    agent to pick on their behalf, or asks what they prefer.
                    "Which library should I use?"  "What theme do I prefer?"

                    procedure, a default or a standing instruction.
                    "How should logs be handled after deployment?"

                    comply.
                    "Do I consider Acme a trusted source?"  "Do I need to verify?"
